"Up" = month-end close at least the stated percent above average cost per share including fees, checked only at month ends. ## Verdicts

Better than its base only if CAGR beats B0 in 2015-2020, 2021-2025 and the holdout, with full-period max drawdown no worse than B0 + 5 points. A MOM1 pass still needs the TDEX evidence gate.

- F5 P1 5% then top up to 10% once when up 5%: FAIL (not above B0 in 2015-2020; not above B0 in 2021-2025; not above B0 in Holdout 2011-04 to 2014)
- F5 P2 10% then add up to 15% once when up 10%: PASS
- MOM1 P1 5% then top up to 10% once when up 5%: FAIL (not above B0 in 2015-2020; not above B0 in Holdout 2011-04 to 2014)
- MOM1 P2 10% then add up to 15% once when up 10%: FAIL (not above B0 in 2015-2020)

## Caveats

- The universe is today's SET50 members with history; delisted stocks are missing.
- Checks run only at month ends; the interviewed trader adds intraday and uses leverage (block trades, futures), which a cash account cannot replicate.
- P1 leaves half of each new name's budget in cash until the top-up; that cash earns nothing here.
- The holdout ranks fewer names (stocks listed later have no early prices).
